## Skyblock Custom Advancement Tree

Skyblock features custom advancement trees that track progress across mining, crafting, farming, mechanics, and other miscellaneous things.

### Visible Advancements

**Was that supposed to happen?**: Convert a lava source block into obsidian. This advancement is for humilliating purposes for players who mess up their cobblestone generator. If you messed up yours and need to restart your island you can run `/skyblock reset (--confirm)`. For more information on skyblock commands check [Player Commands](/commands/player-commands/).

**Dirt duper**: Till coarse dirt with any Hoe. In vanilla skyblock dirt is a renewable block. You can obtain gravel by bartering with piglins. You can craft 4 coarse dirt with 2 dirt + 2 gravel. You can then till the coarse dirt and get 4 dirt blocks, essentially converting gold ingots into gravel and then into dirt.

**Lava duper**: Fill a cauldron with lava using pointed dripstone. You can get pointed dripstone from wandering traders.

**Unlocking gold**: Craft a Gold Ingot.

**Wood you believe it?**: Gather every type of log (`oak`, `spruce`, `birch`, `jungle`, `acacia`, `dark_oak`, `mangrove`, `cherry`, `crimson_stem`, `warped_stem`, `pale_oak_log`).

**Bamboozeled**: Obtain Bamboo.

**Mining Progression Chain**:
  - *You rock*: 100 stone/cobble mined
  - *Don't take me for granite*: 1,000 stone/cobble mined
  - *You're a rock-star*: 10,000 stone/cobble mined
  - *Cobble-stoned*: 100,000 stone/cobble mined
  - *I AM ONE WITH THE STONE*: 1,000,000 stone/cobble mined
  - *Go touch grass*: 10,000,000 stone/cobble mined. **Reward**: Cobble trophy.

## Secret Hidden Advancements

Hidden advancements do not appear in the advancements UI until unlocked. Achieving any hidden advancement unlocks the `Hidden advancements` tab.

- **THERE'S NO FUCKING WAY**: Obtain Bedrock. For more information on how to obtain bedrock check [Custom Loot Tables](/features/custom-loot-tables/).
- **Renewable sand!**: Kill Husk mob.
